# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Changes are found through `:page/edit-time`, so deleting or moving a block
  sends the page again.
- The settings say that the chosen pages feed the website chatbot too.
- Left-out pages and daily notes stay out of block references.
- Saved chats keep visitor text as plain words, never links, tags or embeds.
- The Asyntai address must use https.
- The sync map is saved once per bulk run, and not at all for an unchanged page.

- Ask panel with Ask, Chats and Leads tabs.
- Send pages by title prefix, with automatic sync on edit, rename and delete.
- Block references are sent as the text they point to.
- Six command palette commands and the `/Asyntai ask` slash command.
